# Camp outlines and category artwork

Camp 1: 19 lessons, display Level 1–19. Camp 2: 26 lessons, display Level 20–45. Existing access checks use a course-local ordinal 1–26 for Camp 2; `displayLevelNumber` maps all presentation labels. No enrollments are granted by initializing the outline.

All course tabs can be browsed without enrollment. Lesson buttons, reader opening, progress mutations, and existing database material policies still enforce enrollment, unlocked level, and expiry. The public outline metadata policy already existed and is unchanged.

Badges: copper Pre-POSN, silver Camp 1, gold Camp 2. All 12 camp banners were generated individually with the built-in image generation tool, then resized to 1200 pixels wide and compressed as JPEG for the site. Original PNGs are retained in `deliverables/camp-art-sources/`. These are decorative historical science apparatus images, not instructional diagrams. No equations, labels or direction arrows are generated.
